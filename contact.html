Chemical Buyer FAQ

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)? ↓

For bulk commodity chemicals, MOQ is typically 1 metric ton or one pallet (4 drums / 1 IBC). For high-value fine specialty reagents, packaging starts from 25kg or 25L.

Are Certificates of Analysis (COA) included? ↓

Yes, lot-specific COA documents certified by our ISO 17025 accredited analytical lab are emailed before shipping and physically attached to every container pallet.
